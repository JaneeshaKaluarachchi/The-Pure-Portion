imported raw white have decreased slightly.

Paddy

increased by 9% in Ampara. Meanwhile, 
keeri samba paddy prices in Polonnaruwa

(red) prices increased by 8% in Matara 
district. However, the highest price decrease 
for paddy was reported in Dehiattakandiya 
by 8% for keeri samaba. 
Compared to the same time period last year, 
the average prices of short grain (samba) 
and long grain (white and red) have risen in 
the range of 5%-45% in major producing 
areas. However, the average price of keeri 
samba has decreased marginally in several 
regions.

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating 
to wholesale and retail prices of selected 
food commodities in nine selected markets in 
and around Colombo and main markets in 
the major producing areas.  
The information is analyzed and presented as 
prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, and 
a comparison of weekly prices. The changes 
in prices reported are always with price 
prevailed during the previous week unless 
otherwise stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale 
price information is twice a week ( i.e., 
Tuesday and Friday) while with retail prices, 
it is thrice a week (i.e., Friday, Monday, and 
Tuesday).
