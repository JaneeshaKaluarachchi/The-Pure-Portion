Vegetable 
Wholesale 
Prices of most of upcountry vegetables 
have increased while prices of most low 
country vegetables have decreased. 
All the upcountry vegetable prices have 
increased with the decreased supply from 
major producing areas, specifically, carrot 
reported the highest as 61%, following 
prices of all the other vegetables increased 
in the range of 3% -36%. Further, with the 
decreased supply from all the major 
producing areas, price of tomato has 
increased by 18% and price of capsicum 
has increased 8%.  
Considering low-country vegetables, most 
of the vegetable varieties experienced price 
decrease. Particul arly, price of cucumber 
has decreased by 17%, following prices of 
ash plantain and brinjal have decreased by 
13% and 11% respectively. Prices of 
pumpkin, ladies fingers, luffa and snake 
gourd have decreased in the range of 4% - 
8%. However, prices of drums tick has 
increased by 34% with availability of 
quality stocks from Puttalam and Jaffna 
areas, and prices of long beans and bitter 
gourd have increased slightly. In addition, 
price of green chilies has decreased by 12% 
while price of lime has increased by 23%. 
Compared to the previous year, most of the 
vegetable prices have increased, with 
tomato reported the highest as 1443%. 
 
Retail 
Retail prices of most of upcountry 
vegetable varieties increased in line with 
the upward wholesale trend. Considering 
up-country vegetables, the highest price 
increase was reported for carrot at                 
Rs.116 .00/kg. However, the price of beans 
and beetroot increased by Rs. 18.00 /kg and 
Rs.7.00 /kg respectively.  
 
Considering the low -country vegetable 
varieties, the highest decreased of 
drumstick, ladies fingers and capsicum               
by Rs.125.00/kg , Rs.84.00/kg and 
Rs.80.00/kg respectively. 
Compared to the same period last year, 
prices of most varieties have increased, 
while a notable price increment of 411%

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating 
to wholesale and retail prices of selected 
food commodities in nine selected markets 
in and around Colombo and main markets 
in the major producing areas.  
The information is analyzed and presented 
as prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, 
and a comparison of weekly prices. The 
changes in prices reported are always with 
price prevailed during the previous week 
unless otherwise stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale 
price information is twice a week ( i.e., 
Tuesday and Friday) while with retail 
prices, it is thrice a week ( i.e., Friday, 
Monday, and Tuesday).
