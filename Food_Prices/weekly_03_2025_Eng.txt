paddy. Subsequently, average price of long 
grain (white) paddy also have decline by

Vegetable 
Wholesale 
Prices of most of the up-country vegetables 
have increased due to decrease supply with 
extreme weather conditions while prices of 
most of the low-country vegetables have 
decreased with increased supply from 
major producing areas. 
Most of the upcountry vegetable prices are 
increased, specifically, carrot reported the 
highest as 25% with decreased supply from 
Nuwara Eliya area, following prices of 
leeks, and cabbage have increased by 22% 
and 10% respectively. Price of green beans 
has increased slightly. However, price of 
raddish, beetroot, and knol -khol have 
decreased in the range of 1% -12% with 
availability of stocks. Further, price of 
tomato and capsicum have increased by 
7% and 6% respectively. 
Considering low-country vegetables, most 
of the vegetable varieties experienced price 
decrease with increased supply from 
Rajanganaya, Embilipitiya and Dambulla 
areas. Particularly, for bitter gourd (15%), 
snake gourd (13%), cucumber (11%) and 
pumpkin (10%) prices decreased 
significantly. Prices of brinjal and ash 
plantain has decreased by 7%. However, 
prices of ladies fingers, long beans and 
luffa have increased in the range of 12% - 
30%. In addition, price of green chilies has 
increased by 20% while price of lime has 
decreased slightly. 
Compared to the same period of last years, 
most vegetable prices have decreased, with 
carrot reported the highest as 84%.

Fruits 
Wholesale 
The wholesale price of many fruits have 
fallen due to increased supply and 
decreased demand.  
The end of the harvest season and low 
stocks in term of quality and quantity have 
led to a 15% and 10% decreased by the 
prices of wood apple and local orange. 
Among the babana varieties, ambul and 
kolikuttu recorded a 12% and 10% 
decrease in prices due to overstocking, 
heavy rainfall and reduced demand. 
Prices of all sizes of pineapples have 
decreased, with the highest price decrease 
recorded at 10% for small pineapple. 
The price of red grapes imported from 
China has increased slightly due to limited 
supplies. 
Wholesale prices for the most of fruits have 
increased compared to last year, 152% 
increase was recorded for karthakolomban.

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating to 
wholesale and retail prices of selected food 
commodities in nine selected markets in and 
around Colombo and main markets in the major 
producing areas.  
The information is analyzed and presented as 
prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, and a 
comparison of weekly prices. The changes in 
prices reported are always with price prevailed 
during the previous week unless otherwise 
stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale price 
information is twice a week (i.e., Tuesday and 
Friday) while with retail prices, it is thrice a 
week (i.e., Friday, Monday, and Tuesday).
