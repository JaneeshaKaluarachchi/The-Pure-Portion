Vegetable 
Wholesale 
Prices of most of the vegetables have 
increased due to decreased supply during 
extreme weather conditions. 
Most of the upcountry vegetable prices 
have increased, specifically, green beans 
reported the highest as 70% with decreased 
supply from Balangoda area, followed by 
prices of raddish, carrot and leeks 
increased in the range of 4% -16%. 
However, prices of beetroot, knol-khol and 
cabbage have decreased in the range of             
4%-17% with supplies from Puttalam area. 
Further, prices of tomato and capsicum 
have increased by 11% and 52% 
respectively with due to low supplies. 
Considering low-country vegetables, most 
of the vegetable varieties experienced a 
price increase. Particularly, ladies fingers 
(34%), snake gourd (30%), bitter gourd 
(23%), long beans (14%) and cucumber 
(12%) prices have increased significantly. 
Prices of brinjal and ash plantain have 
increased by 11% and 7% respectively. 
However, price of pumpkin has decreased 
by 8%. In addition, price of green chilies 
has decreased by 15% while price of lime 
has decreased 25%. 
Compared to the previous year, most 
vegetable prices have decreased, with 
carrot reported the highest as 75%. 
 
Retail 
Retail prices of most of the vegetable 
varieties increased in line with the upward 
wholesale trend. In up-country vegetables, 
the highest price increase was reported for 
beans at Rs.43.00/kg, followed by carrots 
at Rs.4.00/kg.  
 
In low-country vegetable varieties, 
capsicum prices significantly increased to 
Rs.198.00/kg compared to the previous 
week. Snake gourd prices showed a 
significant price increment of                        
Rs. 65.00/kg. However, lime has shown a 
notable price drop at Rs.78.00/kg. Green 
chilies (Rs.86.00/kg) showed a significant 
price decrease compared to last week.

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating to 
wholesale and retail prices of selected food 
commodities in nine selected markets in and 
around Colombo and main markets in the major 
producing areas.  
The information is analyzed and presented as 
prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, and a 
comparison of weekly prices. The changes in 
prices reported are always with price prevailed 
during the previous week unless otherwise 
stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale price 
information is twice a week (i.e., Tuesday and 
Friday) while with retail prices, it is thrice a 
week (i.e., Friday, Monday, and Tuesday).
