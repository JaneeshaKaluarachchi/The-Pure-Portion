Prices of most local rice varieties have not

at the market with the following average

• Imported ponni samba - Rs.228.00/kg 
• Imported nadu - Rs.218.00/kg 
• Imported raw white - Rs.215.00/kg

7% for both nadu grade I and II. In the 
contrast the price of keeri samba has 
decreased by 24%. 
 
Retail

increased. Prices of local raw red and white

the current retail prices of all the rice 
varieties except for samba grade I and keeri 
samba have increased with the highest price 
increase of 42% was recorded for raw red.

Vegetable 
Wholesale 
Prices of some of the up -country 
vegetables have increased due to decrease 
supply with extreme weather conditions 
while prices of most of the low-country 
vegetables have decreased with increased 
supply from major producing areas. 
Among the upcountry vegetables, the 
highest price increase noted for beans by 
22% with decreased supply from 
Balangoda area. Prices of knol-khol, radish 
and beetroot have increased slightly. 
However, prices of carrot, leeks, and 
cabbage have decreased in the range of 
25%-29% with availability of low-quality 
stocks. Further, price of tomato  has 
decreased by 40% with increased supply 
from Sooriyawewa, Kandy and 
Bandarawela areas. Price of capsicum has 
also decreased by 10%. 
 
Considering low-country vegetables, most 
of the vegetable varieties experienced price 
decrease with increased supply from 
Rajanganaya, Embilipitiya and Dambulla 
areas. Particularly, for snake gourd (28%), 
bitter gourd (14%), brinjal (14 %) and 
pumpkin (13%) prices decreased 
significantly. Prices of long beans and luffa 
have decreased in the range of 1% - 6%. 
However, prices of ladies fingers, 
cucumber and ash plantain have increased 
in the range of 6% - 13%. In addition, price 
of green chilies has increased slightly 
while price of lime has decreased by 16%. 
  
Compared to the same period of last year, 
most vegetable prices have decreased with 
carrot reported the highest as 73%.

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating to 
wholesale and retail prices of selected food 
commodities in nine selected markets in and 
around Colombo and main markets in the major 
producing areas.  
The information is analyzed and presented as 
prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, and a 
comparison of weekly prices. The changes in 
prices reported are always with price prevailed 
during the previous week unless otherwise 
stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale price 
information is twice a week (i.e., Tuesday and 
Friday) while with retail prices, it is thrice a 
week (i.e., Friday, Monday, and Tuesday).
