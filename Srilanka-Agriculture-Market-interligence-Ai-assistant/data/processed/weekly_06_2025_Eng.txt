Wholesale 
Price of keeri samba has increased by 2% 
due to unavailability of sufficient stocks 
and the price ranged Rs.255.00-267.00/kg. 
However, the price of nadu, raw red and 
raw white rice varieties have decreased in 
the range of 2%-6% due to availability of 
sufficient stocks. Samba grade I and samba 
grade II were available at the market and 
the average prices were Rs.238.00/kg and 
Rs.232.00/kg respectively. 
Imported rice varieties were also available 
at the market, with the following average 
prices: 
• Imported ponni samba - Rs.228.00/kg 
• Imported nadu - Rs.218.00/kg 
• Imported raw white - Rs.208.00/kg 
Compared to the same period of last year, 
the current wholesale prices of most of the 
rice varieties have increased with the 
highest price increase of 26% for raw red. 
The price of keeri samba has decreased by 
26% compared to the same period of last 
year. 
Retail 
Retail prices of all the types of rice varieties 
except for keeri samba have decreased. 
Among those, the highest price decrease of 
Rs. 42.00/kg was recorded for local raw red. 
Prices of raw red and white rice ranged 
between Rs.220.00-260.00/kg in Colombo 
and suburb markets. The retail price of keeri 
samba has increased by Rs 3.00/kg and the 
price ranged Rs.260.00-280.00/kg. 
 
Compared to the same period of last year, 
the highest price decrease of 30% was 
recorded for keeri samba and the highest 
price increase of 18% was recorded for raw 
red.

price increase of Rs.3.00-9.00/kg for keeri 
samba paddy in those areas and the prices 
ranged Rs.115.00-135.00/kg. Further, the 
average price of long grain white paddy has

Vegetable 
Wholesale 
Prices of most of the vegetables have 
decreased with the increased supply from 
most of the major producing areas. 
Considering up country vegetables, the 
highest price decrease was reported for 
green beans as 21%, followed by beetroot, 
radish and leeks as 13%. However, the 
prices of carrot, cabbage and knol-khol 
have increased in the range of 5%-41% 
with decreased supply from Nuwara Eliya 
and Kandy areas. Further, the price of 
tomato has decreased by 10% with 
availability of stocks from Kandy and the 
price of capsicum has increased by 4%. 
Considering low-country vegetables, most 
of the vegetable varieties experienced price 
decrease with availability of stocks from 
Puttalam, Dambulla and Embilipitiya 
areas. Particularly, for luffa (28%), long 
beans (14%) and ladies fingers (12%), the 
prices have decreased considerably. 
However, the prices of brinjal, ash plantain 
and bitter gourd have increased in the range 
of 8%-28% with limited availability of 
stocks. In addition, the price of green 
chilies has increased by 14% due to 
decreased supply from Jaffna and the price 
of lime has decreased by 12%. 
Compared to the previous year, the 
wholesale prices of most vegetable have 
decreased, with the highest price decrease 
of 76% reported for tomato, followed by 
pumpkin as 52%.

at Rs.48.00/kg. Moreover, the price of 
radish has decreased by   Rs.37.00/kg.   
Considering the low-country vegetable 
varieties, long bean price  showed

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating to 
wholesale and retail prices of selected food 
commodities in nine selected markets in and 
around Colombo and main markets in the major 
producing areas.  
The information is analyzed and presented as 
prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, and a 
comparison of weekly prices. The changes in 
prices reported are always with price prevailed 
during the previous week unless otherwise 
stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale price 
information is twice a week (i.e., Tuesday and 
Friday) while with retail prices, it is thrice a 
week (i.e., Friday, Monday, and Tuesday).
