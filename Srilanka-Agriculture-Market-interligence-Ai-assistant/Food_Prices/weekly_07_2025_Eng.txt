Vegetable 
Wholesale 
Prices of most of the vegetables have 
decreased with the increased supply from 
most of the major producing areas. 
Most of the up-country vegetable prices are 
decreased, specifically, green beans 
reported the highest as 31% with increased 
supply from Balangoda and Keppetipola 
areas, followed by beetroot (18%) due to 
increased supply from Jaffna areas. Prices 
of raddish and knol-khol have increased 
slightly. However, price of cabbage (33%), 
leeks (22%) and carrot (7%) have 
increased due to decreased supply from 
Nuwara Eliya and Kandy areas. Further, 
price of tomato has decreased by 19% with 
availability of stocks from Ka ndy and 
Jaffna areas and price of capsicum has 
increased by 8%. 
Considering low country vegetables, the 
highest price decrease was reported for 
long beans as 29% due to price of green 
beans has decreased, followed by ladies 
fingers (16%). Prices of snake gourd, ash 
plantain and cucumber have decreased in 
the range of 3%-14%. However, prices of 
bitter gourd (13%), pumpkin (10%), Luffa 
(5%) and brinjal (4%) have increased with 
decreased supply from major producing 
areas. In addition, price of green chilies has 
increased by 13% due to decreased supply 
from major producing areas while price of 
lime has slightly increased by 3%. 
Compared to the previous year, most 
vegetable prices have decreased, with 
tomato reported the highest as 81%.

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating to 
wholesale and retail prices of selected food 
commodities in nine selected markets in and 
around Colombo and main markets in the major 
producing areas.  
The information is analyzed and presented as 
prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, and a 
comparison of weekly prices. The changes in 
prices reported are always with price prevailed 
during the previous week unless otherwise 
stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale price 
information is twice a week (i.e., Tuesday and 
Friday) while with retail prices, it is thrice a 
week (i.e., Friday, Monday, and Tuesday).
