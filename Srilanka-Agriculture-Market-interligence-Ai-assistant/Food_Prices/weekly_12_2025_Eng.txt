Vegetable 
Wholesale 
Prices of most of upcountry vegetables 
have increased due to decrease supply 
while prices of most low country 
vegetables have decreased due to increase 
supply from major producing areas.  
 
Most of the upcountry vegetable prices 
have increased, specifically, knol -khol 
reported the highest as 17% with decreased 
supply from Nuwara Eliya area, following 
prices of beetroot, green beans and rad ish 
have increased in the range of 3% -15%. 
However, price of carrot, leeks and 
cabbage have decreased in the range of           
5%-30%. Fur ther, price of tomato has 
decreased by 25% with increased supply 
from Sooriyawewa, Balangoda and Kandy 
areas and capsicum has increased by 5% 
with decreased supply from Jaffna area.  
 
Considering low-country vegetables, most 
of the vegetable varieties experienced price 
decrease. Particularly, price of cucumber 
has decreased by 30% with availability of 
stocks, following prices of luffa has 
decreased by 12%. Prices of bitter gourd, 
ash plantain, long beans, and ladies fingers 
have decreased in the range of 3%  -7%. 
However, price of brinjal has increased by 
68% with decreased supply from major 
producing areas and price of snake gourd 
has increased by 15%. In addition, price of 
green chilies has decreased by 39% with 
increased supply from major producing 
areas while price of lime has decreased by 
17%.  
Compared to the previous year, most 
vegetable prices have increased, with 
ladies fingers reported the highest as 231%. 
 
Retail

vegetables, the highest price decrease was

Considering the low -country vegetable 
varieties, luffa prices showed a significant

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating 
to wholesale and retail prices of selected 
food commodities in nine selected markets 
in and around Colombo and main markets 
in the major producing areas.  
The information is analyzed an d presented 
as prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, 
and a comparison of weekly prices. The 
changes in prices reported are always with 
price prevailed during the previous week 
unless otherwise stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale 
price in formation is twice a week ( i.e., 
Tuesday and Friday) while with retail 
prices, it is thrice a week ( i.e., Friday, 
Monday, and Tuesday).
