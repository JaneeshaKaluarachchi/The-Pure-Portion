Wholesale 
Price of most local rice varieties have not 
increased significantly. Price ranges of 
nadu grade I and of raw white                           
were Rs.225.00-230.00/kg and Rs.215.00-
220.00/kg respectively. The raw red rice 
varieties were not available at the market 
due to unavailability of sufficient stocks. 
Imported rice varieties were also available 
at the market, with the following average 
prices: 
• Imported ponni samba - Rs.228.00/kg, 
• Imported nadu and imported raw white - 
Rs.218.00/kg. 
Compared to same period of last year, the 
wholesale prices of most of the rice 
varieties have increased with the highest 
price increase of 7% for both nadu rice and 
raw white. The price of keeri samba has 
decreased by 21% compared to same 
period of last year. 
 
Retail 
The retail prices of all the local rice varieties 
except for local samba grade I have 
increased. Among those a notable increase 
of Rs.12.00/kg was observed for raw red. 
The retail prices of imported samba , 
imported nadu and imported raw white have 
increased by Rs.4.00/kg, Rs.3.00/kg and 
Rs.2.00/kg respectively.  
 
Compared to the same period of last year, 
the current retail prices of all the rice 
varieties except for samba grade I have 
increased with the highest price increase of 
31% recorded for raw red.

Vegetable 
Wholesale 
Prices of most of the vegetables have 
decreased due to increased supply from 
major producing areas. 
Prices of most of the upcountry vegetables 
have decreased, with the highest price 
decrease reported for beans as 53% with 
increased supply from Balangoda area, 
Prices of all the other  up-country 
vegetables, except for beetroot have 
decreased in the range of 2%-16% with 
availability of sufficient stocks. However, 
the price of beetroot has increased by 24% 
with decreased supply from Puttalam and 
Nuwara Eliya area. Further, the price of 
tomato has increased by 49% with 
decreased supply from Sooriyawewa, 
Kandy and Bandarawela areas while the 
price of capsicum has decreased by 28% 
with increased supply from Nuwara Eliya 
area. 
Considering the low-country vegetables, 
the prices most of the vegetable varieties 
experienced price decrease with increased 
supply from Rajanganaya, Dambulla, 
Embilipitiya and Balangoda areas. 
Particularly, for bitter gourd (30%) and 
long beans (22%)  the prices have 
decreased significantly. Prices of all the 
other low country vegetables except for 
snake gourd have decreased in the range of 
4%-20% with availability of stocks. 
However, the price of snake gourd has 
increased by 7% with availability of 
quality stocks from Beruwala area. In 
addition, the prices of green chilies and 
lime have decreased by 19% and 5% 
respectively.  
Compared to the previous years, the prices 
of most of the vegetables have decreased, 
with   the highest price decrease of 62% 
reported for carrot.

EXPLANATORY NOTE  
The Food Commodities Bulletin is a weekly 
publication containing information relating to 
wholesale and retail prices of selected food 
commodities in nine selected markets in and 
around Colombo and main markets in the major 
producing areas.  
The information is analyzed and presented as 
prevailing prices, price ranges, averages, and a 
comparison of weekly prices. The changes in 
prices reported are always with price prevailed 
during the previous week unless otherwise 
stated.  
The frequency of collection of wholesale price 
information is twice a week (i.e., Tuesday and 
Friday) while with retail prices, it is thrice a 
week (i.e., Friday, Monday, and Tuesday).
